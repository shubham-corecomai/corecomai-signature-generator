3. Add it to Gmail

  1. In Gmail, click the gear icon → See all settings → General tab → scroll to Signature → Create new.
  2. Click inside the signature box, click the picture icon in the toolbar, choose Upload, and pick the GIF you downloaded.
  3. Click the picture once and choose Original size.
  4. Optional: with the picture still selected, click the link icon and paste your website address so people can click the signature.
  5. Under Signature defaults pick this signature for new emails and replies, then click Save Changes at the bottom of the page.
  6. Send yourself a test email to see the animation. It may look still while you are writing the email.

Outlook: File → Options → Mail → Signatures → New, then Insert picture. Some email apps (reports say classic Outlook for Windows) show only the first frame, which is the finished signature. The phone numbers and website are part of the picture, so people can’t tap or copy them.

Everything runs in your browser — the details you type are never uploaded or stored.
